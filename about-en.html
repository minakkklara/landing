About Me

Work Experience

I began my career in journalism and TV news, then moved into product companies in 2020, where I grew into a commercial and technical editor. I see my path forward as a copywriter, technical writer, and commercial editor within a product company.

At RuStore (a VK product), I work as a technical writer. I maintain technical documentation for users and developers and write release notes. I also publish technical articles on Habr and work with Markdown and Git.

At Yandex Market, I worked as a B2B Help Center editor. I collaborated closely with product teams, updating and expanding help center articles for partners. I documented processes in the user's personal account and formatted content in Oxygen XML Editor, working with GitHub.

At MoySklad (2020–2022), I worked as a product editor. I collaborated closely with product teams and helped craft in-product UI text. I wrote newsletters (Bitrix24, Unisender) and social media copy about new features. I also authored articles on the platform's capabilities, including guides on marketplace accounting and small-scale production, and maintained documentation in Confluence. Alongside this, I created interview-based publications with industry experts and built and updated landing pages.

Earlier in my career (2008–2020), I worked in journalism and broadcast media — at Forbes.ru, Moskva 24, 360°, VGTRK, Vechernyaya Moskva, and others — as a correspondent, reporter, and editor. I managed teams of up to 10 news editors: distributing tasks, editing copy, and overseeing publishing workflows.
